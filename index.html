PRESENTATION

Vault Heist challenges teams to build secure applications, attack each other, and then cooperate to open a common vault: The Vault.

Inspired by the intense atmosphere of Quidditch and magic, the event combines technical creativity, defensive skills and live offensive strategy.

EVENT OBJECTIVES

Technical Creativity

Foster the creative and technical expression of the participants.

Networking & Talents

Identify emerging talent and provide opportunities for exchange.

Application Security

Raising awareness about Web/App security: live attacks and defense.

Collaboration & Rivalry

Encourage teamwork, even between rival teams.

EVENT PROCEEDING

THE 4 PHASES OF THE VAULT HEIST

Each team goes through four strategic stages to collect keys and attempt to unlock the final Vault.

1
Cipher Round

Each team receives an encrypted code and must crack it to discover their development theme. Providing an answer after the deadline results in a penalty.

2
Construction

The teams build their application, secure it, and carefully hide within it the piece of the Vault key that was assigned to them at the start.

3
Attack & defence

The applications are swapped (3 per team). Try to steal the hidden pieces while simultaneously fixing your own flaws in real time!

4
Vault unlocking

Complete the bonus challenges to earn extra keys. The Vault opens once the required number of keys has been collected.

Obtain Key Parts
✓ EXCHANGE: Trade a piece with another team in exchange for points (from the start).
✓ THEFT: Find a hidden key inside any of the given apps (Attack Phase).
✓ BONUS CHALLENGE: Complete a special challenge to earn an extra coin.
Fair-Play Charter
• Attacks are permitted only against applications assigned within the game environment.
• Strictly forbidden to target infrastructure, the scoreboard, or personal machines.
• The organizers' decisions are final and not subject to appeal.
